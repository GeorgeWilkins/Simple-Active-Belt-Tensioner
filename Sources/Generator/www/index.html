Printables Generator

If you need to customise SABT parts for your sim rig, you can do so here without needing to install or learn FreeCAD. Pick a part to customise from the list below and enter the values that match your requirements. More parts and customisation options will be added over time...

Motor Bracket for Aluminium Profile Frames

This is the bracket that attaches each tensioner motor to your aluminium profile. You'll need to print two of these, but they are symmetrical; so there is no need to mirror one of the prints.

The design can be customised to be mounted to the face or edge/corner of your aluminium profile. In both cases it requires two T-slots to bolt onto; either on the same or perpendicular faces, depending on your chosen mounting option.

Source File: Motor Brackets/For Aluminium Profile (Parametric).FCStd

Base Angle (90~180°)

This bracket supports two types of mounting. Use 90° for edge/corner mounting and 180° for face mounting. Alternatively use any angle within that range for more unusual rig frames with angled profile

Base Width (>=60mm)

How wide the bracket base should be. For face mounting this is often equal to your outer profile width. For edge/corner mounting this is measured around the corner, rather than directly between the tips of the legs (such that each leg is Base Width x 0.5 wide)

Hole Spacing (>=30mm)

The distance between the mounting holes. For face mounting this is usually equal to the profile slot spacing. For edge/corner mounting this is measured around the corner, so the hole inset from the profile edge is Hole Spacing x 0.5

Hole Diameter (6~10mm)

The size of the bolt holes for mounting (typically 8mm for M8). A modest 0.2mm clearance is automatically added when generating the holes (for printer inaccuracies), so you don't need to account for that in the value you provide

Clearance Height (55~200mm)

How far from the profile the cord should exit the motor pulley. This must be at least 55mm for the motor drum to clear the profile; higher values will create weaker brackets unless Base Width is also increased (and even then, greater heights will flex under load)

Motor Bracket for Round Tube Frames

This is the bracket that attaches each tensioner motor to your round tubular frame. You'll need to print two of these; one of which must be mirrored in your slicer.

The design can be customised to the exact diameter of your frame tubing, which is typically 50mm, 2", 25mm or 1".

For frame designs where the rear tube touches the floor (supporting the frame weight), this bracket design incorporates a foot to distribute the load.

Source File: Motor Brackets/For Round Tube (Parametric).FCStd

Tube Diameter (20~51mm)

The outer diameter of your tubing. Ideally you should measure this accurately rather than relying on the manufacturer's stated value.

Clearance Height (55~200mm)

How far from the tube the cord should exit the motor pulley. This must be at least 55mm for the motor drum to clear the tube.
