# Controller Case
This protects your motor controller board from damage while providing convenient mounting lugs for your rig.

![Parts](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4ea2460f-4882-4683-becd-81270c0dc2df)

The two halves are secured together with `4x` `M2.5x16mm` screws and `4x` `M2.5` nuts from the standard kit needed by the project.

How you attach this to your rig, and what hardware you use to do so, is up to you.
