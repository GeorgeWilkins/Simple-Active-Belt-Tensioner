# Back-Driving Protection Case
This protects your back-driving protection unit from damage or shorting out.

![Parts](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b58f0ed9-aeff-4ad4-85a9-291ec5b8c857)

The two halves are secured together with `4x` `M2.5x16mm` screws and `4x` `M2.5` nuts from the standard kit needed by the project.
