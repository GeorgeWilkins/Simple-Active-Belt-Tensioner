# Pulley
![Pulley](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bfb17203-2bb1-4e9f-a6be-a8232badc16d)

The pulley consists of three printed parts:
- [Hub](Hub.step): The centre spool to which the cord is attached and wraps around
- [Cover](Cover.step): The outer shell that keeps the cord wrapped neatly inside and prevents tangling
- [Face](Face.step): The outer central cover that secures everything together

The large slot in the cover is where the pulley cord exits. The smaller holes are for (optionally) anchoring the pulley cover to your sim rig frame, in situations where the self-orienting feature doesn't work reliably.

Print these in the orientation shown above.
