# Pulleys
These attach to your motors and wind the cord to apply tension to your belts.

![Parts](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fecfe26a-7651-42fb-95fe-5ea35f9e53b7)

## Choosing Your Pulleys
There are two versions of the pulley design available:
- [Non-Anchorable](/Printables/Pulleys/Non-Anchorable.step): A simple self-orienting pulley with a plain exterior
- [Anchorable](/Printables/Pulleys/Anchorable.step): As above, but with additional anchoring holes around the outside (intended for **waist belts** or solving issues with excessive slack)

Both of these designs are secured to your motors with `3x` `M2.5x12mm` screws from the standard kit needed by the project.

Each pulley requires one `6706` (`30x37x4mm`) bearing, which press-fits betwen the outer cover and fascia parts.

The `Non-Anchorable` pulley is the simpler and better-looking design of the two. However it relies on there being at least a little tension in your harness belts at all times in order to self-orient and function correctly.

For **shoulder belts** this is normally fine, since their weight pulls on the cords naturally and keeps slack minimal. For **waist belts** however, this is unlikely to be the case and you may run into situations where the cords tangle. This can also happen if your bearings are stiffer than expected. In such cases the `Anchorable` pulley is recommended, as it solves these issues.
