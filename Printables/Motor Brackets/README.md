# Motor Brackets
These attach the motors to your rig. Since there are various rig frame types, a number of options are available.

(IMAGE HERE)

## Choosing Your Brackets
### For Aluminium Profile (30, 40 & 45 Series)
Choose from the `For Aluminium Profile (...).step` bracket designs. All of these are **symmetrical**, so you don't need to worry about mirroring parts.

Select the appropriate size and height for your profile type. It is recommended that you use the _measuring tool_ within your slicing software to verify hole spacing before printing as these are automatically generated and have not all been verified by printing.

There are also `Edge` and `Face` variants that allow you to mount the bracket on the edge or face of the aluminium profile, respectively.

### For Tubular Steel (1" & 2")
Choose from the `For Round Tube (...).step` bracket designs. All of these are **non-symmetrical**, so you **do** need to mirror parts in your slicer.

## Quantity
You'll need one bracket per motor. Multi-part brackets include all needed parts and can be _printed-in-place_.

Note the orientation and symmetry of your chosen bracket design before printing, as some require mirroring in your slicer.

For **four-motor** configurations, you will need to print _four_ of your chosen bracket. Consider what orientation you are going to mount your motors in. For a paired configuration (using two motors per belt), you typically want the paired motors facing each other, so their pulleys and cords are close together.

## Customisation
If you need to change any of the dimensions, you can open the **FreeCAD** [source files](/Sources/Printables/Motor%20Brackets/) and adjust the `VarSet...` parameters accordingly, then export to a `STEP` file.

Basic customisations can also be made to the `STEP` files inside most slicing software.

For more advanced customisations, get in touch with your requirements.
