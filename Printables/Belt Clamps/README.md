# Belt Clamps
**SABT** uses cord wrapped around pulleys to apply force to your harness. We must therefore attach this cord to your harness belts.

These belt clamps are designed to do just that. Some of these integrate an additional idler pulley to achieve increased force output.

Many **SABT** users have chosen to simply tie their belt cords to their existing steel anchoring plates; bypassing the need for these printed clamps altogether. If you already have anchoring plates attached to your harness, you may wish to do this instead.

## Choosing Your Clamps
Since there are a variety of belt types and terminations, a few options are available:
- [For Belt Ends](/Printables/Belt%20Clamps/For%20Belt%20Ends.step): This is designed to clamp onto an unterminated 2" belt (suitable for bare belt ends, or belts you are happy to shorten to the ideal length)
- [For Belt Loops](/Printables/Belt%20Clamps/For%20Belt%20Loops.step): This has an open slot through which a 2" belt (up to 4mm thick) can be looped and secured back on itself using a standard belt buckle (likely to have come with your harness)
- [For Belt Passthrough](/Printables/Belt%20Clamps/For%20Belt%20Passthrough.step): This is designed to clamp anywhere along a 2" belt without needing to shorten or terminate the belt itself

All of the above clamp designs use `4x` `M2.5x10mm` screws and `4x` `M2.5` nuts from the standard kit needed by the project, so require no additional hardware.

### Motor Configurations
All of the above designs have three cord holes. This allows you to use either **one motor** (the middle hole) or **two motors** (the outer holes) per belt. Motor pairing doubles the force applied to a single belt, a the cost of not being able to have **active waist belts**.

## Alternatives
### Idler-Equipped Belt Clamps
If you'd like to achieve a _higher tension output_ without resorting to pairing motors, you can consider using _idler-equipped_ belt clamps. These clamps incorporate an additional idler pulley around which the cord is routed and then anchored to the motor bracket; effectively increasing the mechanical advantage and allowing for greater tension (_almost double_) to be applied to the belts at the expense of slightly reduced responsiveness:
- [For Belt Ends (With Idler Pulley)](/Printables/Belt%20Clamps/For%20Belt%20Ends%20(With%20Idler%20Pulley).step)
- [For Belt Loops (With Idler Pulley)](/Printables/Belt%20Clamps/For%20Belt%20Loops%20(With%20Idler%20Pulley).step)

These idler pulley clamp designs use `5x` `M2.5x12mm` screws and `5x` `M2.5` nuts from the standard kit needed by the project. They also require `2x` `682XZZ` `2.5x6x2.5mm` bearings per clamp.

All [Motor Bracket](/Printables/Motor%20Brackets/) designs feature a place to tie off the anchored end of the cord when using these idler-equipped belt clamps, so you won't need to re-print your motor brackets if you choose to try this out at a later date.
